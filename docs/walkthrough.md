## Transcript

### 00:00 — Start in English

Welcome to Gloss Reader. Choose English in the top bar. Open a public article link, paste your own text, or explore the built-in sample.

### 00:09 — Open your own text

To paste an article, add a title and your text, then choose Start reading. Blank lines become paragraphs. Two hash signs create a section heading.

### 00:20 — Read without distractions

Here is the built-in sample essay. The main text stays in the center, with a table of contents on the left and help in the margin.

### 00:28 — See the main idea

At a glance opens a short summary. The sample summary is included. Summaries of your own articles require a connected model.

### 00:37 — Select a passage

Drag across a sentence. The selection toolbar lets you ask, explain, define, highlight, or add a note. Highlight keeps the passage in your notebook.

### 00:48 — Explore the explanation

Try a sample explanation to see help beside the text. This answer is clearly labeled as a prewritten sample. Free-form questions need an AI connection.

### 00:58 — Keep your own thought

Save the explanation, or add your own note. Write why the passage matters to you. Both the quote and your thought are saved together.

### 01:07 — Revisit and export

The notebook collects your highlights, notes, and saved explanations. Filter the list, return to a passage, or export everything as a Markdown file.

### 01:18 — Make the page your own

Open Appearance to change the paper color, font, text size, line spacing, or highlight color. Your choices stay saved in this browser.

### 01:28 — Stay with the text

Focus mode clears the surrounding controls. Press Escape to return. The speaker button can read the article using your browser voices.

### 01:37 — Connect a model

In AI settings, choose OpenAI with your own key, or an Ollama model running locally. Questions send the article and selected passage to your chosen model. No key is shown in this demo.

### 01:50 — Read. Ask. Keep the thought.

Your articles and notes stay in this browser. Export a copy when you want a backup. Read, ask, and keep the thought.

## About the sample

The video uses the included sample essay and an original short pasted text. The sample explanation is prewritten and clearly labelled in the app and narration. The video shows provider settings without entering a key or sending a live model request.
